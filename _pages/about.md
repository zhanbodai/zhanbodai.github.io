I'm Zhanbo Dai, a Master of Science student in [Data Science at the University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://datascience.wisc.edu/), graduating in May 2027. I hold a B.S. in Economics with a Math emphasis and a minor in Data Science from UW–Madison.

My academic interests sit at the intersection of **statistical learning theory, big data systems, and applied machine learning**. Recent coursework includes Statistical Learning, Statistical Inference, Big Data Systems (Hadoop, Spark), Nonlinear Optimization, and Deep Learning. I'm especially drawn to the mathematical foundations behind modern ML methods, from kernel methods and concentration inequalities to deep learning architectures.

## What I'm working on

- Building distributed data pipelines with Spark and Hive on HDFS, integrated with LLMs for natural-language querying
- Exploring statistical learning theory: VC dimension, Rademacher complexity, RKHS, and kernel methods
- Compiling course notes and cheatsheets across statistics, ML, and deep learning (see [Projects](/portfolio/))

## Background

Before my MS, I spent summers as a marketing intern at Shanghai Xinhua Lian (analyzing survey data with Python and R), a financial research intern at CITIC Bank (supply-chain analysis of McDonald's in China), and an intern at Bank of China.
